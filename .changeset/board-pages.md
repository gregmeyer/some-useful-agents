Pulse and named dashboards are boards.

`/pulse` and `/dashboards/<id>` now render as boards: tiles you place and size on a grid with **Edit**, saved for every browser and arrangeable by agents, with Pulse's unplaced agents in a tray grouped by recent use. Each tile keeps its ⚙ configure and × (hide from Pulse, or remove from a dashboard), and its ● palette is now saved with the board. **✨ Suggest a layout** replaces Improve layout on these pages, and **Save as pack** exports the board's arrangement. The previous layout is one switch away for this release: Settings → Appearance → **Show Pulse and dashboards as boards**.
