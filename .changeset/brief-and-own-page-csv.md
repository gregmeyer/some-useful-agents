A brief to share, and each option's own page in the CSV.

**Download / share** in a notebook's header now has **Open the brief**: one polished, self-contained page of the notebook (the goal, where it stands, the decision or shortlist with each option's facts, own page and checks, the limits and done-whens), in light and dark, that you can download or hand to Claude to publish as an artifact. The CSV gains **Own page** (each option's own listing, only when it has one) and **Found on** (the search page, when that's all it has).
