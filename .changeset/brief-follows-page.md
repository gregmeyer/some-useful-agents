A notebook's brief prints the page it decided on.

For a notebook whose page was drawn for it (a Meeting briefing, a report), Download / share now prints that page: its parts in the same order and editorial treatments, as one self-contained file (a cover with their photo and its source, ruled facts, the numbered agenda, what they said with sources, the research at the end). The extended brief adds every quote, every fact with its page, and each option in full. A saved brief carries the person's photo and the company's logo inside it. Shortlist notebooks keep the shortlist brief.
