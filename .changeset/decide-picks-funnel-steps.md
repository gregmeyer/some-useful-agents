Decide picks from the options still in the running, and the funnel follows a notebook's steps.

**Decide…** now lists every option still in the running, best first, with its fit, price or measure and how many checks it passes, to tick (or, when the decide step wants one, to choose with a radio button). Why is optional. The picks are marked chosen, the decision says what was chosen, and the decide step is marked done (it never was before). A notebook with steps shows its funnel as the steps (Found → Facts sourced → Checked → Chosen), counted from each option's facts, checks and picks, instead of hand-moved stages that nothing advanced.
