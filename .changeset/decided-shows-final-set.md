A decided notebook shows its final set (on the page and in its CSV), and Decide… shows pictures.

Decide… rows show each option's picture when it has one. After you decide, the shortlist shows only the chosen options (untick "Only chosen" for the rest), Where it stands says "Decided: 8 chosen, of 16 in the running", and the funnel's Chosen stage counts every pick, not only picks that passed every earlier step.

Download CSV asks which options: Chosen, In the running, or Everything with ruled out; after a decision it gives the chosen ones. The shortlist's "CSV of these N" exports exactly what it shows, filters and all. The CSV has a Chosen column, and on notebooks with steps its Stage column is how far each option got (Found, Facts sourced, Checked, Chosen).
