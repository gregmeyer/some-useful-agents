Home's Ask sua offers ways to start a notebook, from your templates and the notebooks you've made.

A Start row joins Run, Build, Fix and Look up: your templates first (sua starts one from the template; one that changes each time ends where you say who or what), then the kinds of notebook you've made that no template covers ("Prepare for a meeting with …", "Help me choose …", "Plan …"), then a meeting and a report, up to four. A kind opens New notebook with its sentence started.
