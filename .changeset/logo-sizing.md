Company logos are sized by their shape, so they read.

A wordmark logo (wide) fills its frame's width and gets a wider tile in the notebook's header; a square app icon sits smaller, with room around it. The brief's thumbnails are wider, and a drawing's thumbnail zooms in on its initials. Notebook pictures are now checked again each time they're shown (a 304 when unchanged), so a picture that changes at the same address shows at once. Core adds `imageSize`, `imageShape`, `NotebookStore.photoShape` and `imageShape` on view options and covers.
