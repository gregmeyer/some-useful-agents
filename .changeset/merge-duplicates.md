Duplicate options: the same address is the same option, and you (or sua) can merge the rest.

An option found again at the same address in the notebook's link field (ignoring "www.", trailing slashes and tracking parameters) is now the same option, however the agent keyed it, as long as the year in its title, its main measure (within 5%) and its company agree, since a search-results page several listings point at isn't one listing. An option's page has **Merge with…** (likely copies first), and sua can merge options when you say they're the same.
