A notebook step can narrow the list without closing the notebook.

A new step kind, narrow ("Decide which cars stay in the running"), is yours like decide. While it's the current step, **Narrow…** in the header lists the options still in, with pictures and facts. Keep the ones that stay, and the rest are ruled out (with your reason, or "Not kept at …"). The step is done and the notebook stays open for the next one. The funnel counts it as Kept, and sua's notebook drafter can now lay out a narrow step before decide when a goal asks for a shortlist first.
