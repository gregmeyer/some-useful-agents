A notebook started without steps gets them from sua.

When you use Skip the draft, or a draft comes back with no steps, sua lays out the notebook's steps itself once setup is done. It uses the notebook's goal, the facts it notes and your agents, and changes nothing else. sua's first message in the notebook's conversation lists the steps. The drafter now also names the facts a source step looks up.
