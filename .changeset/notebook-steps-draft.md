sua lays out a notebook's steps, from your sentence or the conversation.

The notebook draft now includes steps (find, source, check, decide), each with the one of your agents whose description fits that job. On the draft you can rename a step, pick its agent or remove it, and starting the notebook saves the steps. In a notebook's conversation, when the work needs several stages, sua proposes the steps as a `notebook-steps` card instead of running each stage itself. The card previews every step and its agent, and can start step 1. Only installed agents can be named, and none is changed.
