A notebook decides its page again once its first step is done, unless you changed it.

A new notebook picks its page before it has anything. Once its first step reaches its goal (who it's about learned, its first options found), it decides again and says so in its conversation. It only does this while the page is still its own pick: a page you changed (Undo, Back to Shortlist), one from a card you approved, or a template's stays. A step added in front of a notebook's steps no longer renumbers the others.
