Decide… can tick sua's picks.

When sua recommends which options to choose in a notebook's conversation, it now marks them (a `picks` list in its notebook update, replacing its earlier picks). In Decide…, those rows carry a "sua" badge, and "Tick sua's picks (N)" ticks exactly them.
