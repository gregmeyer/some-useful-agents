sua's replies can include a widget too.

When an answer has several numbers, compares a few things, or lists items that share fields, sua can put a widget (a table, stats, facts) under its reply in Home, the sua panel and inbox threads. It's checked like any view, kept with the reply, and drawn above the reply's links.
