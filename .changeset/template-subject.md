Save as a template opens a page where you make it fit any time, and say what changes each time.

Edit → Save as a template… now shows the template's name, its texts to edit (what it's for, limits, done-whens, checks, step titles) and **What changes each time**: what to ask ("Who you're meeting (a person or company)") and what it was in this notebook ("Dana Reyes", guessed from the title). Wherever the texts named it they say `{subject}`, and starting a notebook from the template fills it in: New notebook has a box for it beside Start, and sua asks for it first otherwise. Checks also keep up to 120 characters, cut on a word, instead of being clipped mid-word at 60.
